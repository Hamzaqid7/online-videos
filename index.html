About NovaStream Custom Video Engine

Privacy First

NovaStream Player is a client-side media playback web engine designed for high performance, zero data-upload buffering, and custom playback control. Unlike traditional streaming platforms, local video files selected from your computer or phone are processed completely inside your device’s browser memory without uploading to any remote server.

100% Private Local Memory

Videos never leave your local machine. Blob URLs render content directly on hardware GPU acceleration.

Sub-Frame Speed Control

Micro-adjust speed from 0.25x slow motion up to 3.0x rapid analysis in 0.05x increments.

Multi-Format Support

Play MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV, and AVI seamlessly without installing third-party codecs.
